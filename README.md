Study of load characteristic and magnetic characteristic of DC.

# Fuse Rating Calculation for Field and Armature:

## No-load test:
Fuse rating = 10% of rated current (full-load current).

## Load test:
Fuse rating = 125% of rated current (full-load current).

# Precautions

1. Motor-side field rheostat should be kept at minimum resistance position.
2. Generator-side field rheostat should be kept at maximum resistance position.
3. Starter should be in OFF position before switching on the supply.
4. The DPST switch must be kept open.

# Procedure for Open Circuit Test

1. Connections are given as per the circuit diagram.
2. The motor is started with the help of a three-point starter.
3. Adjust the motor speed to rated speed by adjusting the motor field rheostat when the generator is disconnected from the load by DPST switch 2.
4. By varying the generator field rheostat gradually, the open-circuit voltage (E₀) and corresponding field current (Iᶠ) are tabulated up to 125% of the rated voltage of the generator.
5. The motor is switched off using DPST switch 1 after bringing all the rheostats to their initial positions.

# Procedure for Load Test

1. Connections are given as per the circuit diagram.
2. The prime mover is started with the help of a three-point starter and is made to run at rated speed when the generator is disconnected from the load by DPST switch 2.
3. By varying the generator field rheostat gradually, the rated voltage (Eᵍ) is obtained.
4. The ammeter and voltmeter readings are observed at no-load condition.
5. The ammeter and voltmeter readings are observed for different loads up to the rated current by closing DPST switch 2.
6. After tabulating all the readings, the load is brought to its initial position.
7. The motor is switched off using DPST switch 1 after bringing all the rheostats to their initial positions.

# Result

The open-circuit and load tests on the DC shunt generator were studied, and the corresponding characteristics were obtained. The open-circuit characteristic shows an increase in generated voltage with increasing field current, while the load characteristic shows a decrease in terminal voltage with increasing load current.
